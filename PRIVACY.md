# Privacy

The `todo` plugin collects no data and makes no network requests.

Your TODO items stay in two local Markdown files: `~/.claude/todo.md` and `TODO.md` at your project's root. The plugin reads and writes only these files. It does not send their content, or anything else, to the author or to any third party.

When you ask Claude about your TODO list, Claude reads the list through the plugin's `todo` tool. That content then goes to Claude like any other conversation content, under the terms of your Claude account.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/wlsdnen/claude-code-todo/issues.
